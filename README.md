# Our Family Bookshelf

A kid-friendly catalog of the books on our family's e-readers: covers, short descriptions, shelves, tags,
reading levels and a "Surprise me!" button.

It's a single static page (`index.html`) plus cover thumbnails in `covers/`. No build step.

Most of the books are public-domain classics from [Standard Ebooks](https://standardebooks.org) and
[Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org). Cover thumbnails are shown for identification only.
